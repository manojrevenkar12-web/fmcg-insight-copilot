## OTIF (on time in full)
A delivery is OTIF when it arrives on or before the promised date and contains at least 98% of the ordered quantity. Supplier OTIF is the share of deliveries that meet both conditions. A delivery that is on time but short counts as a miss.

## Purchase price variance (PPV)
The difference between what we paid and the contract price, multiplied by the delivered quantity. A positive PPV is unfavourable: we paid more than agreed. PPV is reported in euros and is owned by Procurement.

## Contract price
The price per kilogram agreed with suppliers in the annual material contracts. It is held in ERP master data and changes only through a contract amendment.

## OEE (overall equipment effectiveness)
OEE combines availability (share of planned time the line ran), performance (speed versus the ideal rate) and quality (share of good output). It is calculated from totals as good output divided by the ideal output for the planned time. Averaging OEE percentages across lines is wrong because it overweights small lines.

## Availability
The share of planned production time during which a line was running. Planned maintenance, changeovers and cleaning reduce availability but are planned downtime.

## Unplanned downtime
Downtime caused by mechanical breakdowns, electrical faults, material shortages or quality holds. Maintenance, changeovers and cleaning are planned downtime and are excluded.

## Scrap rate
The share of produced kilograms that could not be sold. Restarts after breakdowns typically raise scrap for several shifts.

## Promotion share
Promotional discounts as a share of gross revenue. It shows how much revenue is given away to drive volume. Net revenue is gross revenue minus promotional discounts.

## Net price per kg
Net revenue divided by kilograms sold. It moves with promotions, product mix and list-price changes.

## Return rate
The share of delivered cases that customers sent back, for example for damaged packaging or short shelf life.

## Data layers
Raw extracts land unchanged in the landing zone. The staging layer types and standardises them, the quality layer tests every row and quarantines failures with a reason, and the consumption layer holds the clean facts and dimensions that reports and the copilot read.
