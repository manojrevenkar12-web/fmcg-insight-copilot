## Sunflower oil prices, April 2026 onwards
Following a poor harvest in the Black Sea region, spot prices for sunflower oil rose sharply from April 2026. Deliveries from Danube Oils and Black Sea Seeds have been invoiced 18–30% above contract price since then. Procurement is renegotiating the oil contracts for 2027 and evaluating a partial switch to rapeseed oil blends.

## Baltic Agro delivery problems, July–August 2026
Baltic Agro's main trucking contractor became insolvent in early July 2026. Deliveries of potatoes and corn grits arrived late and short through July and August. A replacement carrier has been in place since 1 September 2026 and delivery performance has recovered.

## PL01 line 2 gearbox failures, August 2026
Line 2 at the Vistula plant (PL01) suffered repeated gearbox failures during August 2026, causing long unplanned stops and higher scrap after each restart. A replacement gearbox was installed on 2 September 2026. Maintenance has added the gearbox to the vibration-monitoring programme.

## Netherlands summer promotion, Q3 2026
The "Summer Crunch" campaign ran with both Dutch retail customers from July to September 2026, with deep price promotions on Crunchwell chips. Volume rose, but the promotion share of gross revenue in the Netherlands roughly tripled. Revenue Management will review the campaign's net profitability in October.

## Data quality, extract of 1 October 2026
The October extract contained duplicate purchase-order lines from a re-sent ERP file, a handful of deliveries with negative quantities or missing suppliers, downtime events without a reason code and sales lines with an unknown SKU. All were quarantined and reported to the data owners; none affect the consumption layer.
